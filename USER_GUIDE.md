# User Guide — PaperRockets

Welcome to PaperRockets, a 3D painting studio for tablets and phones.

**[Read the complete user guide →](./COMPLETE_USER_GUIDE.md)**

The guide is organized in five task-based parts:

1. **First Run & The 60-Second Sketch** — Your first painted stroke, the four screen zones, and the Finger-Pen Golden Rule
2. **Drawing & Shaping** — The three brushes (Tube, Ribbon, Star Dust), Magic FX, and shape snapping
3. **Moving Around & the Navigator** — Camera controls, Flat Screen vs 3D World modes, and view snapping
4. **Stage, Light & Tracing** — Sky presets, reference image overlays, and mirror symmetry
5. **Pro Studio & Delivery** — Layers, model export, Draco compression, WebXR, and keyboard shortcuts

Start with Part 1 if it's your first time. Jump to the section you need.
